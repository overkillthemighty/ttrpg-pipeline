Mic test

Opens every microphone at once, the way a multi-mic browser recorder would, and checks whether each one really delivers two separate channels. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

  1. Press Start test and allow microphone access.
  2. Have each person with a lav talk for a few seconds, one at a time (or tap each lav mic). Then say something into the room mic.
  3. After about 30 seconds press Copy results and paste them back to Claude.
